About Nibhyam Interiors

Transforming houses into beautiful living spaces since 2015.

Our Story

Nibhyam Interiors was founded with a simple yet powerful vision — to make premium interior design accessible, affordable, and hassle-free for every homeowner. What started as a small design studio in Greater Noida has grown into a trusted name in the interior design industry across Delhi NCR.

With over 250+ projects completed and a track record of delivering on time, we have earned the trust of hundreds of families who now live in spaces that truly reflect their personality and lifestyle.

We are not just designers — we are manufacturers. With our own factory and production facility, we control every aspect of quality, from material sourcing to final installation. This means better prices, faster delivery, and uncompromising quality for our clients.

Our Mission

To transform every house into a beautiful, functional home by delivering world-class interior design solutions with transparency, creativity, and craftsmanship. We believe that great design should be accessible to everyone, and we work tirelessly to make that a reality.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative interior design company in India, known for our commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and design excellence. We aim to set new standards in the industry by combining traditional craftsmanship with modern design thinking.
